About Me

Hi, I'm Jenya, a Lead Frontend Developer based in Pennsylvania, USA, originally from Israel. I’ve spent the last decade building scalable web applications and leading frontend initiatives for data-driven products.

My core strengths are in Angular, React, and TypeScript, with a strong focus on micro frontends, monorepos (Nx), and clean, modular UI architectures. I enjoy solving complex problems, improving performance, and making large codebases easier to work with.

Beyond the code, I love mentoring engineers, collaborating with product and design, and shaping frontend best practices so teams can move faster without sacrificing quality.
